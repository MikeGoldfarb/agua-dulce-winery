The Offering

Two assets, one escrow. A wine business that is already running, and the 90 acres it runs on.

Most wine-country offerings ask a buyer to build something. This one is already built, already staffed and already selling, sixteen years into its current ownership.

Agua Dulce Winery sits on Sierra Highway in northern Los Angeles County, close enough to the city for a Saturday crowd and far enough out to feel like somewhere else entirely. Eighty of its ninety acres are planted. Behind the vines is a full production facility with a bottling line, a barrel warehouse and capacity for 25,000 cases a year. In front of them is a 9,300 square foot tasting room, an events barn, a covered pavilion and parking for more than a hundred cars. A five-bedroom manor house sits among the vines.

The result is a property that can be taken in several directions. Continue the operation as it stands. Scale the production behind an existing label. Or keep the land and the improvements and put a new name on the door.
